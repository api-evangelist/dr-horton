# D.R. Horton (dr-horton)

D.R. Horton is the largest homebuilder by volume in the United States, building and selling single-family homes in dozens of states across multiple price points. The company is a Fortune 500 enterprise with affiliated financial services including DHI Mortgage, DHI Title, and DRH Insurance.
